What this is

A growing library about Jesus and the life of his Kingdom.

The Right Side Up is a digital publishing studio. We turn sermons, studies, and long-form teaching into beautiful, theologically grounded publications made to be read slowly, revisited, and shared.

01

Begin with Jesus

Each publication begins with Scripture and helps us see Jesus, his way, and the world he is making new.

02

Make room to notice

The writing and design are intentionally spacious—inviting attention to become contemplation, and contemplation to become formation.

03

Offer it as a gift

No ads, popups, paywalls, or manufactured urgency. Everything here is made to be freely read and generously shared.
